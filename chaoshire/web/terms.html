Purpose and permitted use

ChaosHire is an open-source educational prototype for investigating fairness risks in automated hiring decisions. You may explore the built-in synthetic fixtures and upload synthetic or properly anonymized decision data you have the right to use. Do not submit real applicant, employee, or other sensitive personal information to the public demo.

What the service does not do

The scores, counterfactual experiments, mitigation simulations, and release gates are research and engineering aids, not legal advice, an employment decision, a discrimination finding, or a fairness certification. Do not use this public service to make or automatically enforce real hiring decisions. Human review, valid ground truth, and applicable legal advice are required for any production assessment.

Shared features and safety

An appeal may appear in a public review queue after imperfect automated redaction. An operator-authenticated upload can publish aggregate results and its name to a public history; an anonymous upload is computed and returned to its uploader without publication. You are responsible for avoiding identifying information and for using the service lawfully. Do not attack, flood, impersonate users, bypass rate limits, or attempt to access another party's data.

Availability, code, and changes

The free demo may sleep, restart, lose ephemeral history, or change without notice. It is provided “as is”, without a promise of accuracy, fitness for a particular purpose, or uninterrupted access. Privacy and contact
