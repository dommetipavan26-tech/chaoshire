# TalentFit v4 model update — 28 October 2026

## Scope and safety boundary

This change updates the bundled **synthetic reference model** `trained`, now displayed as TalentFit v4. It does not alter the data generator, model family, merit features, protected/proxy exclusion, or score formula. It changes the model's single global operating point and adds reproducible policy evaluation and a synthetic-label accuracy floor to the release gate.

**This is not a hiring recommendation, a legal-compliance finding, or evidence of fair outcomes for real applicants.** All training, tuning, and holdout data are generated by the repository's deterministic simulator. Do not use this model or its threshold to screen real people.

## Policy and reproducibility

- Fit: deterministic scikit-learn logistic regression on the seed-29, 1,000-row demo fixture, using only `skills`, `experience`, `education`, and `certs`.
- Excluded from prediction: gender, ethnicity, age band, college prestige, and career-gap features. The loader refuses protected or proxy weights. There is one cutoff for all candidates; per-group cutoffs remain unavailable.
- Candidate false-rejection cost ratios: 2.0, 2.5, 3.0, 3.5, and 4.0; corresponding probability cutoffs are `1 / (1 + cost)`.
- Tuning: 49 generated populations, seeds 1–50 excluding the training seed 29. Choose the highest mean fairness risk score among candidates with at least 0.80 mean accuracy against the synthetic qualification label.
- Selected policy: cost ratio **3:1**, probability cutoff **0.25**. It is the best-scoring candidate on that grid that meets the mean-accuracy guardrail; 3.5 and 4.0 score higher but fall below the guardrail.
- Independent holdout: 50 generated populations, seeds 51–100, disjoint from both training and tuning. Coefficients are frozen; the holdout does not retune the policy.
- Reproduce with `python -m chaoshire train --policy-study`, `python -m chaoshire train --holdout`, and `python -m chaoshire train --check`.

The artifact now identifies model version 4 and stores a digest for both coefficients and decision-policy metadata. The loader rejects stale versions, stale policies, digest mismatches, protected weights, and proxy weights. The release gate separately defaults to at least **80% accuracy against the bundled synthetic label**; this is a non-degeneracy guard, not a real-world quality estimate.

This result is explicitly a trade-off, not “fairness solved.” Part of the fairness-score increase is mechanical: a more permissive global cutoff narrows group selection-rate ratios. Twenty-six percent of the held-out populations still miss the worst-DI four-fifths check, and the mean false-positive rate is higher. The held-out populations are repetitions of one known synthetic generator, not external or real-world validation.

## Limitations and next steps

The `qualified` field is a synthetic merit-threshold label, not a validated hiring outcome. The study cannot measure construct validity, real group distributions, label bias, calibration under shift, job-relatedness, disparate impact in a real workforce, or legal compliance. The fairness-risk score is a screening indicator and should be reviewed alongside uncertainty, acceptance volume, accuracy, recall, and false-positive rates—not maximized in isolation.

Any future use with actual applicant data would require a separate, representative and lawfully collected validation set; job-specific domain ownership of error costs; pre-registered evaluation and subgroup analysis; human review and appeal paths; independent legal, privacy and security review; and an authorized deployment decision. No real applicant data, deployment, or release was performed for this update.

## Follow-up evaluation record — 29 October 2026

The original v4 coefficients and global cutoff remain frozen. The release gate and
build-time diagnostics have since been extended to frozen baseline/stress holdouts,
bootstrap uncertainty, global-cutoff sensitivity, a Pareto frontier with optional
FPR/recall constraints, probability-calibration diagnostics, and a model card. See
the [TalentFit v4 model card](MODEL-CARD-TALENTFIT-V4.md) for the current governance
record and results. The follow-up is synthetic evaluation only; it does not change
the model into a validated employment system and includes no deployment or release.
